03 / Research

From physical models to prediction.

A system does not become predictable simply because we have more data.

I am interested in the space where physical laws, probability and machine learning meet.

CURRENT QUESTION

Can limited experimental data be combined with governing physics to construct models that predict behaviour — while also understanding the uncertainty in those predictions?
01
Physics
Governing equations, conservation laws, constitutive relations and boundary conditions.
02
Experiments
Measurements provide observations of the physical system.
03
Probability
Quantifying uncertainty, variation and the confidence associated with inference.
04
Machine Learning
Learning useful representations and relationships from computational and experimental data.
05
Physics-Informed ML
Incorporating physical constraints into data-driven models.
06
Prediction
Moving from describing what happened toward estimating what happens next.

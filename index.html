01 / ABOUT

Research begins with a question worth understanding.

My interests revolve around understanding how heat and fluid flow behave inside engineering systems, and how computational methods can help turn that understanding into useful predictions.

My current research focuses on microchannel flow boiling, combining experiments and simulation to study complex two-phase thermal transport.

Alongside thermal-fluid research, I am exploring probability, machine learning and physics-informed approaches for engineering prediction.

01 — ABOUT

Building an understanding from physics first.

My work is centred around thermal engineering, particularly problems involving heat transfer, fluid flow and multiphase transport.

I am currently pursuing an M.Tech in Thermal Engineering at IIT Madras, where my research focuses on microchannel flow boiling through experiments and simulation.

My broader interests include computational fluid dynamics, thermal management, scientific machine learning, physics-informed modelling and advanced thermal systems.

Research philosophy

Start with the physics. Build the experiment. Simulate the system. Then use data and computation to discover what the equations alone cannot easily reveal.

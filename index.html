01 / ABOUT

Questions first. Physics second. Computation follows.

I am interested in understanding thermal systems not simply through equations, but through the relationship between physical models, experiments, computation and data.

How does heat move? How does geometry change a flow? Where does prediction become possible?

My academic work began with conventional heat transfer and computational fluid dynamics and has gradually moved toward more complex thermal-fluid problems.

My current research focuses on microchannel flow boiling, combining experiments and simulation to understand multiphase thermal transport.

Alongside thermal engineering, I am developing stronger foundations in probability, statistics, machine learning and physics-informed modelling.

Simulation
